PPimarket Privacy Policy

Global Pi-Powered Marketplace

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PPimarket, a marketplace designed to connect buyers and sellers. This Privacy Policy explains how PPimarket may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our application.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, PPimarket may process:

  • Pi username or account identifier.
  • Profile information and profile photographs.
  • Product descriptions, prices, and listing photographs.
  • Order and transaction status information.
  • Delivery information provided by users.
  • Messages sent to customer support.
  • Technical information required for security and operation.

The actual information collected depends on the features enabled in the deployed application.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate user accounts and marketplace listings.
  • Connect buyers and sellers.
  • Display products and services.
  • Verify payment status where payment features are enabled.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Prevent fraud and protect platform security.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Pi Network and Blockchain

PPimarket may use Pi Network authentication and payment services. When you use these features, relevant account identifiers and transaction information may be processed by Pi Network and its services under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Transactions recorded on a public blockchain may be visible publicly and may not be removable by PPimarket.

PPimarket will never ask you to provide your Pi wallet passphrase or private keys.

5. Product Listings and Public Information

Information you publish in a marketplace listing, including product photographs, descriptions, prices, and seller details, may be visible to other users.

Do not upload confidential documents, wallet secrets, or information you do not want to make public.

6. Third-Party Services

PPimarket may use third-party services for hosting, image storage, authentication, payments, and technical operations. These providers may process information necessary to provide their services.

7. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the marketplace, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, protect security, and meet legal obligations.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held about you. Some records may need to be retained for legal or security reasons.

10. Children's Privacy

PPimarket is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the service in their jurisdiction.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
